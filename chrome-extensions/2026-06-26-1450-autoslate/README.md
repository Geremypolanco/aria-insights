# AutoSlate
*Manage tabs with AI-powered productivity*

AutoSlate is a Chrome extension that helps content creators, marketers, and developers manage multiple tabs and notes. With AutoSlate, you can group and organize your tabs, generate research suggestions, take notes, and automate to-do lists. Try AutoSlate today and boost your productivity! AutoSlate is free to download and use, with optional in-app purchases for premium features. The free version includes basic features, while the premium version adds advanced features such as AI-powered research suggestions and automated to-do list generation. You can upgrade to the premium version for just $9 USD.

- AI-powered research suggestions
- Note-taking and tagging
- Automated to-do list generation
